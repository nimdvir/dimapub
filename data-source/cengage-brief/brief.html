What this book is

Courseware that teaches business students to turn data into decisions: a textbook, an interactive reader, guided builds, labs, auto-graded quizzes and a semester project, built to run as one course. Students organize data, query it with SQL, check the answers, present the evidence and recommend an action, with AI as an assistant whose work they learn to check.

Primary course
The applied business database or MIS data-management course.
Also fits
The required business-core data or IS course, through a shorter route in the same chapters; selected modules for accounting information systems and analytics foundations.

How it is different

  • Starts with the business. Chapter 1 opens with performance, goals and KPIs, followed by data literacy and organizing information.
  • Uses a database before designing one. Students query a supplied database, then model and build their own.
  • Ends in a decision. Analytics, visualization and a full Power BI chapter lead to a decision memo.
  • Makes checking a habit. From Chapter 5, students accept, revise or reject AI-assisted work and explain why.

What exists today

Seven years of teaching this course to more than 4,000 students shaped the book. Version 1 was tested in class in summer 2026; version 2 is in use with 500 students in fall 2026. The full textbook is live as an interactive edition, with a Let’s Build, a lab and an auto-graded quiz in every chapter, an instructor dashboard, two running cases and an Access route.
