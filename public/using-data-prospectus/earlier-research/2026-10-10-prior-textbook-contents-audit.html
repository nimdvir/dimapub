What the counts mean

One school counts once per title. A school naming the same book in multiple courses, editions, semesters or duplicate files still contributes one to that book’s count. “Schools” means naming or assigning the book in this collection; required/main, optional-only and listing-only counts show the strength of that evidence.

The largest observed count is 3 schools, shared by Database Systems: The Complete Book, Fundamentals of Database Systems, Database Management Systems and Principles of Database Management. Coronel’s Database Systems appears at 2 schools; Pearson’s Modern Database Management also appears at 2 schools.

This is a bounded, mixed historical sample, not a national market-share estimate or a claim of current adoption. 28 of 31 schools name a title included here; absence from a syllabus does not mean a school uses no book. The 2024–26 column identifies recent evidence and includes drafts/master documents.

Counting rules, scope and edition differences
  • Required/main: explicitly required, designated main text, or listed as the course text. “Listed main” and draft texts remain visibly distinct from explicit requirements in the evidence table.
  • Optional only: schools with recommendations, suggestions, references or instructor-source citations and no required/main occurrence for that title.
  • Listing only: remaining schools whose evidence is a departmental list or course-material listing rather than a main-text assignment. These three columns sum to the school count.
  • Courses: unique school + course-label pairs; files: unique saved source IDs. Neither is the popularity ranking. School totals across titles are not additive.
  • Different editions of the same established title are combined. Distinct Access versions, customized zyBooks titles and ambiguous titles remain separate.
  • Technical graduate references, software companions and open texts are included for completeness. Use the type filter to examine the database textbook set.
  • Publisher groups follow verified publisher/imprint identities; parent ownership is shown where relevant. Groups are ordered by distinct schools across their titles. Within each group, titles sort by school count descending, then alphabetically for ties.
  • Outlines cover every numbered chapter or major unit of the stated reference edition, with a separate organization summary and relevant appendix notes. Chapter descriptions are paraphrased; source links expose detailed original contents. They are not full section-by-section reproductions.
  • The organization summaries interpret the published contents. A newer reference edition does not establish that a school adopted it, and its outline can differ from the cited historical edition.
