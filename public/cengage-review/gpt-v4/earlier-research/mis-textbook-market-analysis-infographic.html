Strategic Market Analysis

A New Era for MIS Database Education

The landscape of Management Information Systems (MIS) is shifting from monolithic relational theory toward cloud-native, applied environments. Modern business graduates must move beyond "understanding" data to "building" solutions. This analysis defines the opportunity for a textbook that bridges the gap between deep theory and programmatic business application using Access, VBA, and SQL.

Rising Demand in MIS

Data from AACSB highlights a 5.7% growth in business school enrollments since 2020, with a significant resurgence in "career-aligned" technical skills like database management and business analytics.

Key Takeaway: The market for IS-specific learning materials is expanding alongside the corporate need for data-literate managers.
