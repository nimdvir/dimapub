What to take from where

Strengths and known problems, including your review notes where you gave them.

GPT v3 Classical review

Take from here

  • Four research sections, each with its own PDF link on the banner
  • Searchable 63-document course register and 60-title textbook catalogue
  • Student cost table inside the textbook research
  • Earlier interactive research exhibits and a sourced reference register
  • The version you reviewed line by line (blue headers, cover and links on top)

Watch out for

  • Superseded figures, corrected later: 330,000 reach proxy, 88%, "more than six years", "tested MySQL"
  • No U.S. map, regional cards or dual-bar platform chart; no page guide or reading nav
  • Names internal drafts ("Stabilized 14-chapter proposal", "book-2027 candidate")
  • In this copy only: 21 links to Google Drive syllabus copies and GitHub were unlinked (text kept)

Claude site Single-page prospectus

Take from here

  • School list by region (New York, East Coast, National) with a U.S. map and institution cards
  • Dual-bar "which databases they use" chart, which you called very good
  • Inline chapter outline early in the page
  • Compact: 1.1 MB, ten sections

Watch out for

  • No PDF links, no industry research, no searchable registers, no references
  • No technology, assessment or report-library sections
  • Superseded figures: 10,500, 330,000, 88%, "more than six years", "tested MySQL"; old email alias nimrod.dvir@albany.edu
  • In this copy only: a noindex tag was added (the original had none)
